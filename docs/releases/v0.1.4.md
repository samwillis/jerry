# Jerry 0.1.4

- Share journal photographs with an existing Jerry On Line connection. The Mac uploads past and future photos from shared expeditions after you choose **Share Journal Photographs…**.
- Local directions keep remote missions enabled. The Journal stays open during a mission and moves behind other windows if it covers a control Jerry needs.
- Jerry tries another reachable anchor when overlapping windows block his grappling shot. He reads Accessibility controls in batches so the animation can update during screen inspection.
- Old failures stop resurfacing when you refresh JOL. You can dismiss new failure notices.

If you self-host JOL, build and redeploy it from the v0.1.4 source to use the new photo sharing option. [JOL hosting guide](https://github.com/samwillis/jerry/blob/v0.1.4/docs/JOL_SETUP.md).

## Download

Unzip **Jerry-v0.1.4-macos-universal.zip**, quit your previous copy of Jerry, and move **Jerry.app** to Applications.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Includes Apple Silicon and Intel binaries. Signed by Flutterbox Ltd. and notarised by Apple. **SHA256SUMS.txt** contains the archive checksum.
