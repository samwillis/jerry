# Jerry 0.1.2 — Expedition journals

Jerry’s journal now feels more like a scrapbook of his adventures.

- Expeditions are saved automatically on your Mac and can be resumed after restarting.
- Jerry writes first-person field notes and gives new expeditions whimsical names.
- Local milestone photographs include Jerry in the moment, presented as taped, tilted Polaroids with handwritten captions.
- Your prompts and expandable technical details appear as typewritten paper inserts. Jerry’s handwriting uses bundled Caveat.
- Grappling shots stay within 35° of vertical, tightened to 10° from the screen floor. Jerry walks into a suitable launch position instead of firing across the desktop.
- Take Control and Ask Jerry global shortcuts can be changed in Settings.
- Settings and the journal have tidier layouts and native window controls; ropes follow per-window corner radii more closely.

Jerry opens the macOS Accessibility Keyboard through physical interaction with Accessibility settings when he needs to type. Computer tasks remain experimental and may need steering.

## Download

Download **Jerry-v0.1.2-macos-universal.zip**, unzip it, quit your previous copy and move **Jerry.app** to Applications.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Includes Apple Silicon and Intel binaries; execution has been tested on the development Apple Silicon Mac, not Intel hardware or every supported macOS version.

Signed by Flutterbox Ltd. and notarised by Apple. macOS may still request desktop or Keychain access when replacing an earlier build. Credentials remain in Keychain. Journals and photographs stay local; AI image sharing remains a separate setting.

The attached **SHA256SUMS.txt** contains the download checksum. Jerry is an experimental preview.
